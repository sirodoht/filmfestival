The challenge

Small films.
Big cats.

01

Cats

The theme is cats. Real cats, imagined cats, cat people, cat mysteries, cats with wings, cats and dogs. Don’t be limited. Be wild.

02

90 sec

Aim for your film to be 90 seconds. Not a strict requirement, just don’t make one that’s 10 minutes or 3 hours.

03

Made today

Shot, edited, premièred in one afternoon. Shot across London (anywhere you want as long as you’re back by 3:30pm), edited on your phone, premièred on our TV.
